Help centre

Quick answers about getting a quote and using your account.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the form in the Contact section. We call you within one working day, then arrange a free site visit.

How do I track my enquiry?

Create an account or sign in with the same email you used on the form. Your enquiries and their status (new, contacted, quoted, won, closed) appear under My account.

I did not get the verification email

Check your spam folder, then open My account and tap Resend.

I forgot my password

Go to the sign-in page, type your email and tap Forgot password. We send you a reset link.

How do I book a site visit?

Use the calendar in the Book a visit section. Pick a day and a time, and we confirm by phone.

Where can I see my project progress?

Sign in with the email you gave us. Your stage, weekly photos, payment schedule and receipts appear under My account. You also get a bell notification when we post something.

How do I report a problem?

Open your project under My account and use Report an issue. You can add a photo, and our reply appears on the same page.

How does the referral programme work?

Open Refer and earn, copy your link and share it. You can follow each referral there.

Why is there no fixed price per square foot?

Material and labour costs change from week to week. We quote on current rates after seeing your plot and plan, and every quote carries its date.

How accurate is the cost estimator?

It gives an indicative range based on area, building type, finish and the rates we last updated. You can print or save it as a PDF. The final quote follows a site visit and drawings.

How do payments work?

Payments are linked to completed stages and written into your agreement.
