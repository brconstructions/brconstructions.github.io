FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Call us or visit the Help centre.

How long does a house take to build?

About 8 to 10 months for 2,000 sq ft after approvals. Your agreement carries dated stages.

Why is there no fixed price per square foot?

Material and labour costs change from week to week, so we quote on current rates after seeing your plot and plan. The quote lists materials and labour separately, and any change is agreed with you in writing before work continues.

Do you handle building permits?

Yes. We prepare the drawings, submit them and follow them up.

Can I visit the site during construction?

Always. We also send weekly photo updates so you can follow along remotely.

How do payments work?

Payments are linked to completed stages. The schedule is written into your agreement.

Do you build from my architect’s plans?

Yes. We can build from your drawings or supervise the project for you.

What warranty do I get?
