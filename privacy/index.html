Privacy

Short version: we only collect what we need to reply to you and run your account.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry: your name, phone number, optional email, project type and message. When you book a visit or ask for a callback: your name, phone, plot address and chosen time. When you create an account: your email address, and your name if you use Google sign-in. If you are a client, we also keep your project progress, photos, payment schedule and any issues you report, visible only to you and us.

How we use it

To call you back, prepare quotes and show you the status of your enquiries. We do not sell your information.

Who processes it

This website stores enquiries and accounts with Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication). Only you and our admin team can read your enquiries.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics only if you tap Accept on the cookie banner. If you decline, analytics never starts. You can change your choice any time with Cookie settings in the footer.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, contact us at and we will act within a reasonable time.
